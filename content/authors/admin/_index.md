## About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST). My research combines microwave remote sensing, radiative transfer modeling, and deep learning to retrieve soil moisture and vegetation properties and understand the Earth's ecohydrological processes.

I am particularly interested in how soil moisture influences vegetation variability. My work spans physics-informed retrieval methods, harmonization of observations across satellite sensors, and applications to drought and ecosystem monitoring.
